Mini Games Hub: Offline Games

=== SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80) ===
32 fun games in one app: puzzles, arcade, cards, strategy. Play offline!

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Mini Games Hub brings 32 fun games together in one small app. No account needed, most games play offline, and every game saves your best score.

🧩 PUZZLE
• Mahjong – big, clear tiles, a 4-slot tray and endless levels with new tile sets every chapter
• Block Puzzle – place blocks, clear rows and columns
• 2048 – merge the tiles and reach 2048
• Sudoku – Easy, Normal and Hard, a new puzzle every time, notes and hints
• Match 3 – match colorful gems and chain combos
• Minesweeper – three board sizes, flag mode and a safe first tap
• Block Rain – rotate falling blocks, clear lines, hold and ghost piece
• Jigsaw Puzzle – 12, 24 or 40 pieces from colorful pictures

🕹 ARCADE
• Snake – the classic, smooth and fast
• Stack – drop the blocks perfectly and build the tallest tower
• Bubble Shooter – aim, bounce and pop the bubbles
• Rocket Hop – fly your rocket through the asteroid gaps
• Fish World – go fishing, upgrade your rod and raise fish in your own aquarium
• Forest Guardian – survive monster waves, level up and evolve your weapons
• Fox Adventure – run, jump and stomp across 15 levels, collect every star
• Mine Run – dash through three lanes in a mine cart, dodge and collect

🃏 CARDS & CLASSICS
• Memory – flip the cards and find all pairs
• Connect Four – play the computer (three levels) or a friend
• Chess – full rules, three computer levels or play a friend
• Checkers – Turkish draughts vs the computer or a friend
• 8-Ball Pool – realistic physics against the computer

♟ STRATEGY
• Tower Defense – one tower, endless waves, permanent upgrades
• Stock Trader – trade four stocks for 60 days, read the news and grow your fortune
• Countries War – build your country, raise armies and conquer your rivals
• Factory Tycoon – build production chains, sell on the market, fill orders
• Club Boss – run a European football club: tactics, transfers, youth academy, finances and European cups
• Island Tycoon – build hotels, discos and water parks on a tropical island, win price wars and bankrupt your rivals

🎨 CREATIVE & KIDS
• Fashion Show – dress up the model for the theme
• Makeup Salon – do the makeup each customer asks for
• Cake Shop – bake and decorate cakes to order
• Coloring Book – tap to fill, zoom, save your pictures
• Animal Friends – animal pictures, names and sounds for little ones

⭐ FEATURES
• Continue unfinished games where you left off
• Sound effects and vibration (can be turned off)
• Background music (can be turned off)
• Favorites, search and categories
• 10 languages: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, Русский, Dansk, Magyar
• Small download, quick to open

New games are added with every update. Have fun!
