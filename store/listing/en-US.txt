17 fun games in one app: puzzles, arcade, cards, strategy. Play offline!

=== FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000) ===
Mini Games Hub brings 17 fun games together in one small app. No account needed, most games play offline, and every game saves your best score.

🧩 PUZZLE
• Block Puzzle – place blocks, clear rows and columns
• 2048 – merge the tiles and reach 2048
• Sudoku – Easy, Normal and Hard, a new puzzle every time, notes and hints
• Match 3 – match colorful gems and chain combos
• Minesweeper – three board sizes, flag mode and a safe first tap

🕹 ARCADE
• Snake – the classic, smooth and fast
• Stack – drop the blocks perfectly and build the tallest tower
• Bubble Shooter – aim, bounce and pop the bubbles
• Rocket Hop – fly your rocket through the asteroid gaps
• Fish World – go fishing, upgrade your rod and raise fish in your own aquarium

🃏 CARDS & CLASSICS
• Solitaire – Klondike with Draw 1 or Draw 3, undo and auto finish
• Memory – flip the cards and find all pairs
• Connect Four – play the computer (three levels) or a friend

♟ STRATEGY
• Tower Defense – one tower, endless waves, permanent upgrades
• Stock Trader – trade four stocks for 60 days, read the news and grow your fortune
• Countries War – build your country, raise armies and conquer your rivals

⭐ FEATURES
• Earn gold in every game and use it to play
• Continue unfinished games where you left off
• Sound effects and vibration (can be turned off)
• Favorites, search and categories
• 10 languages: English, Türkçe, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, 日本語, Русский, Dansk, Magyar
• Small download, quick to open

New games are added with every update. Have fun!
